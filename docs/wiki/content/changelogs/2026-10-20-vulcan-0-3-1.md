# Changelog - 2026-10-20

## VULCAN 0.3.1: A Smoother First Run

<Badge type="tip" text="Enhancement" /> <Badge type="info" text="Bug Fix" />

**In one line.** `init --preset` and `add --preset` end by telling you which URLs to open, and `add` no longer downloads a preset just to tell you a flag is missing.

## What changed

- **A preset can list the URLs to open.** The optional `urls` key holds paths such as `/api/hello`. `init` and `add` print one `open http://localhost:<port><path>` line per path; a preset without `urls` prints the root. See [Presets](/references/presets).
- **`add` ends with the same `Next:` block as `init`.** It prints `make dev`, then the URLs of the apps in the monorepo.
- **`add` checks flags before it downloads.** Without a terminal, a missing `--app`, a side folder that neither the flag nor `layout` gives, or a frontend with neither `--api` nor `--no-api` is reported first. The side is known from the flags (`--frontend-dir`, `--api` and `--no-api` mean a frontend); when they do not name it, the side checks wait for the download.
- **A new project ignores `*.tsbuildinfo`.** `tsconfig.tsbuildinfo` no longer shows as untracked after the checks.

## Who is affected

- **Preset authors.** Add `urls` when the app does not serve its root. 0.3.0 ignores the key, so nothing breaks.
- **Everyone else.** No action needed.
